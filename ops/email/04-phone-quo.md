# Phone recommendation (replaces NumberBarn)

## Chosen: Quo (formerly OpenPhone)

**Why Quo over NumberBarn:** Real VoIP business line (not just a forwarding park), Windows/Mac/web + iOS/Android apps, SMS, permanent number you own and can port out, call/text from PC, optional ring/forward to your cell.

| Need | Quo |
|------|-----|
| Use from PC | Yes — Windows, Mac, web |
| Forward / ring cell | Yes |
| Permanent / portable | Yes — you keep and can port out |
| SMS | Yes (A2P registration for business texting) |
| Reliability | Established SMB phone product (ex-OpenPhone) |

**Pricing (approx):** Starter ~$15–19/user/mo; local number included; extra numbers ~$5/mo.

**Signup:** https://www.quo.com/
